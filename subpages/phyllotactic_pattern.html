What is a Phyllotactic Pattern?

Phyllotaxis describes the arrangement of leaves on a plant stem and creates a distinctive class of patterns found in nature.

x = r * cos(θ)
y = r * sin(θ)
r = c * sqrt(n)
θ = n * 137.508
