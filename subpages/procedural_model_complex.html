Procedural Modeling — Complex Scene

A Houdini procedural modeling project focused on generating a complex warehouse-style environment through modular node networks, randomized assets,
and repeatable scene construction.

  • Procedural warehouse scene generation in Houdini
  • Node-based systems for conveyors, shelves, pallets, boxes, and lights
  • Texture randomization and paint/copy-stamp placement workflow
